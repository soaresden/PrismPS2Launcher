Here are the CUEs for PlayStation 1.
Prism will look in the root of this directory.

Place the necessary files for the Ember emulator, "ember.elf" and "bios.bin", in this folder.
"mass:/Prism/Roms/CUEs PlayStation 1/ember.elf"
"mass:/Prism/Roms/CUEs PlayStation 1/bios.bin"

For more information regarding using Ember please visit:
https://github.com/Gageformer/Ember

Example:
"mass:/Prism/Roms/CUEs PlayStation 1/Example.cue" - It will find it and list it.

Supported extensions: .cue
