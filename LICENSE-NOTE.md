## The part that is still being settled

Prism started from [RETROLauncher](https://github.com/Spaghetticode-Boon-Tobias/RETROLauncher),
which was published **without a licence file**. Its author has been asked to add
one, or to confirm he is fine with this fork; this note will be updated with his
answer. Until then, his code is here under the terms GitHub grants to forks, his
copyright line stays in this file, and no claim is made over his work beyond
what is needed to keep the project running.

## What the GPL does not cover

Prism ships no BIOS, no game data, and no emulator binary. Enceladus,
RetroArch and its cores, Neutrino, POPStarter, Ember, OPL and wLaunchELF are
separate works under their own licences — see [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md). Some
libretro cores are non-commercial; respect their terms.

The interface design follows the PlayStation-X theme by pajarorrojo, licensed
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The assets in `System/Medias/Pads/psx/` were redrawn from that
description rather than copied, and are offered under the same CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
so that attribution and share-alike carry through.
