Here are the ROMS for Atari Lynk.
RETROLauncher will search in the root of this directory.

Example:
"mass:/RETROLauncher/Roms/Roms Atari Lynk/Example.lnx" - It will find it and list it.
"mass:/RETROLauncher/Roms/Roms Atari Lynk/Example.lyx" - It will find it and list it.
"mass:/RETROLauncher/Roms/Roms Atari Lynk/Example.zip" - It will find it and list it.

Supported extensions: .lnx .lyx .zip (".o" compressed in ".zip" works)
